# Fixtures

Three fictional grants and their direct and indirect costs. Planted: a negotiated 28% rate
charged on 52,000 when the modified total direct cost base (leaving out equipment,
participant support and the subaward above its first 50,000) allows 50,400
(indirect_overcharged), with costs dated after the negotiated rate ended
(rate_expired) and 30,000 of subaward above the cap (subaward_over_cap); a de minimis grant
charged 5,000 where 15% gives 7,500 (indirect_undercharged); and a foundation grant
capped at 10% of total direct costs and charged exactly that.
