# Fixtures

Five fictional expense reports. Names are made up; the mileage rate in the settings is
an example (use the rate in your own policy). Planted: two meals on one day totalling 75
against a 60 per diem (meals_over_per_diem), mileage of 120 for 100 miles at 0.70
(mileage_over_rate), supplies of 90 with no receipt (receipt_missing), a grant-coded
dinner with wine (unallowable_on_grant) sent 75 days later (late_submission), and the
same 50 office purchase claimed on two reports (duplicate_claim).
