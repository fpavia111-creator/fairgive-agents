# Fixtures

Seven fictional members, their dues payments and three membership levels, checked on
2026-11-18. Names and e-mails are made up. Planted: a family membership that ends on
2026-12-05 (expiring_soon), an individual membership that ended in September with no
renewal (lapsed), a patron who paid 400 for a 500 level (paid_wrong_amount), a renewal
paid in October that did not move the end date (paid_not_extended) and an active member
with no payment on file (active_without_payment).
