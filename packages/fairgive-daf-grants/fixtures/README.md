# Fixtures

Eight fictional gifts, six of them grants from donor-advised funds. Sponsor names and
people are made up. Planted: a DAF grant hard-credited to the advisor instead of the
sponsor (daf_hard_credit_to_advisor), a DAF grant that paid for 240 of gala tickets
(daf_paid_benefit), a DAF grant applied to the advisor's own pledge
(daf_applied_to_pledge), an acknowledgement that told the advisor the grant is
deductible (daf_ack_says_deductible) and a DAF gift with no sponsor recorded
(daf_sponsor_unknown). The two ordinary gifts are not listed.
