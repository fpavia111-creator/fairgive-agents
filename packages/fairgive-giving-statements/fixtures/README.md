# Fictional giving records

One year of fictional giving at a fictional church. Names and addresses are made up.
Planted: a giver with no address (giver_missing_address), a refunded gift left out of the
statement (refund_excluded), a gift with no date (gift_no_date), loose offering with no
giver (unattributed, not on any statement), and a December 2025 gift that belongs to the
earlier year's statement.
