# Fixtures

Fictional gifts from nine donors over six fiscal years, with a July-to-June fiscal year,
checked on 2026-11-18 (fiscal 2027). Names are made up. Planted: two donors who gave last
year and not yet this year (lybunt), two who last gave two and three years ago (sybunt),
one new donor, one whose gifts this year are well below last year (downgraded) and one
whose last gift is older than the four-year look-back, so it is not listed.
`exports/` holds a Bloomerang transactions export.
