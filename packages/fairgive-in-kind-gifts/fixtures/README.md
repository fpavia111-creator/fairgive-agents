# Fixtures

A fictional in-kind gift log and the in-kind revenue lines in the ledger. Names are made
up. Planted: furniture logged with no value (no_value), food with no valuation method
(no_valuation_method), a 12,000 painting valued only by its donor with no appraisal noted
(donor_value_only, over_appraisal_threshold), volunteer event help that is not a
specialized skill (services_recognition_check, left out of the recognized total), a van
sold within three years of the gift (disposed_within_3_years) and July office space
logged at 6,000 but booked at 600 (gl_differs).
