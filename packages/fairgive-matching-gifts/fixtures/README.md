# Fixtures

Nine fictional gifts, three fictional employers' matching programs and the match claims,
checked on 2026-11-18. Names are made up. Planted: a 2-to-1 match not claimed yet
(match_not_claimed), a match whose deadline is in ten days (match_deadline_soon), a match
whose deadline passed (match_deadline_passed), a 1,500 match received for a 2,000 claim
(match_received_differs), a claim waiting 136 days (claim_pending_long) and a gift from a
donor whose employer is not in the list (employer_not_in_list). A 20 gift under the
bank's minimum and a gift with no employer are not listed.
