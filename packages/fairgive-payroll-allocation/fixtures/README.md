# Fixtures

One fictional quarter of payroll, effort reports (percent or hours), salary charges by
program and grant, and effort certifications. Names are made up. Planted: an employee
charged 50/50 whose timesheets say 25/75 (charge_differs_from_effort), effort adding up
to 110% and grant charges with no certification (effort_over_100,
certification_missing), 2,000 of pay never charged anywhere (payroll_not_allocated) and
2,000 charged to a grant the employee reported no time on (charged_without_effort).
