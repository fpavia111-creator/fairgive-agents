# Fixtures

Five fictional volunteers and nine hour entries, checked on 2026-11-18 with an example
hourly value of 30 (use the value your organization has chosen). Names are made up.
Planted: a driver whose background check is more than three years old
(background_check_expired), a youth mentor with no background check and no waiver
(background_check_missing, waiver_missing), a 14-hour day (shift_over_max), the same
entry twice (duplicate_entry), an entry dated in December (future_date), and pro bono
accounting with no program (missing_program) that may count as contributed services
(skilled_service_candidate).
