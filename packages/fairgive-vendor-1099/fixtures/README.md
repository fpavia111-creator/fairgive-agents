# Fixtures

Seven fictional vendors and their 2026 payments. Names, taxpayer ids and addresses are
made up. The threshold in the settings (2,000 for payments in 2026) is a setting your
preparer confirms. Planted: a consultant paid 2,500 with no W-9 and no taxpayer id
(needs_1099_no_w9, tin_missing), an LLC with a malformed taxpayer id (tin_malformed),
a law firm paid as a corporation whose fees are still reported (attorney_reportable), a
supply corporation that is usually not reported (corporation_exempt) and a photographer
whose card payments are left out, keeping the total under the threshold.
