# Fixtures

Four fictional grants, their reporting schedules and the reports sent so far, checked on
2026-11-18. Names are made up. Planted: a federal quarterly financial report due on
2026-10-30 that was never sent (overdue), a foundation final report due 90 days after the
grant ended (overdue), an equipment inventory due on 2026-12-01 (due_soon), a quarterly
update sent 15 days late (late_submission) and a new grant with no schedule
(no_schedule). Reports due more than 120 days out are not listed.
