# Fixtures

Eight fictional recurring gift plans and their charges, checked on 2026-11-18. Names and
e-mails are made up. Planted: a card that expires at the end of November
(card_expiring), a card that expired in August while charges still went through
(card_expired), two failed charges in a row (repeated_failures), a monthly plan with no
charge since August (missed_charge), a charge after the plan was cancelled
(charge_after_cancel) and a quarterly charge of 175 on a 150 plan (amount_changed).
`exports/` holds a Stripe payments export with a plan id column added.
