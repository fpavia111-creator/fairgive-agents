# Fixtures

Six fictional grants (five federal) and their spending in fiscal 2026 (calendar year).
Planted: federal spending of 1,040,000, over the 1,000,000 threshold that the settings use
for fiscal years beginning on or after 2024-10-01 (threshold_met); a pass-through award
with no pass-through award id (pass_through_missing_id); a federal grant with no
assistance listing number (missing_aln); a December 2025 cost outside the year and a
foundation grant, both left out. `exports/` holds a Sage Intacct ledger export.
