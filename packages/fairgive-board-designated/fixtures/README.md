# Fixtures

Four fictional board-designated funds, the board resolutions log and the ledger lines.
Planted: roof repairs of 14,000 against 10,000 the board approved
(spending_without_resolution); a 15,000 release the board voted that was never booked
(undesignation_not_recorded) and a gift booked to the donor-restricted net asset account
in a board fund (reported_as_donor_restricted); a fund created with no resolution that
then spent 25,000 and went below zero (designation_without_resolution,
spending_without_resolution, balance_below_zero); and designations of 106,000 against
100,000 of unrestricted net assets (designations_over_unrestricted).
