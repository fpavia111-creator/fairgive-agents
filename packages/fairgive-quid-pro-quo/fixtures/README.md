# Fixtures

Eight fictional payments where donors may have received something back, with what the
receipt said. Names are made up. The insubstantial-benefit figures in the settings are
the IRS figures for 2025 (low-cost article 13.60, minimum payment 68, 2% or 136);
update them each year. Planted: a 150 gala payment with a 60 dinner, no disclosure and a
receipt saying all 150 is deductible (disclosure_missing, deductible_wrong); a tote bag
that is an insubstantial benefit (insubstantial_benefit); a golf outing with no value
(fmv_missing); and an auction item worth more than the payment (benefit_over_payment).
`exports/` holds a Classy (GoFundMe Pro) export with the benefit columns added.
