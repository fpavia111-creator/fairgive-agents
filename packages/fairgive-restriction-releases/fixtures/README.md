# Fixtures

A fictional fund list and one quarter of ledger detail with a fund on every line.
Planted: youth programs spent 5,000 but only 3,000 was released (release_due 2,000);
the music fund released 2,500 against 1,000 of spending and 1,500 of gifts
(release_over_spending and release_over_balance); the fall 2026 scholarship money is
past its release date and still restricted (time_restriction_ended); the fall 2027
scholarship fund released 2,000 before its date (release_before_time). The building
campaign is in order. `exports/` holds a Sage Intacct general ledger detail export.
