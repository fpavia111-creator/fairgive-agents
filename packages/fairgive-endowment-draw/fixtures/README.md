# Fixtures

Three fictional endowment funds, their quarter-end market values and the draws booked for
fiscal 2027, with a 4.5% spending rate on a 12-quarter average to 2026-06-30. Planted:
a music endowment below its corpus at the last quarter end (underwater) whose booked draw
is 625 more than the policy gives (draw_differs), and a library endowment with only six
quarters of history (insufficient_history). The September 2026 value is after the value
date and is not used. Spending rates, averaging and the prudence figure are settings: the
law that applies (often a state's version of UPMIFA) and your own policy decide them.
