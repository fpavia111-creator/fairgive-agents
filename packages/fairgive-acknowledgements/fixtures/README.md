# Fixtures

Fictional gifts and the acknowledgement letter log of one organization, checked on
2026-11-18 with a 250 threshold and a 30-day sending policy. Names and addresses are made
up. Planted: two large gifts with no letter (ack_missing), a letter sent after 36 days
(ack_late), a letter that says 30 for a 300 gift (ack_amount_differs), a letter with no
goods-or-services statement (ack_no_statement), a letter for a refunded gift
(ack_for_refunded_gift) and a gift from eight days ago that is not late yet
(ack_due_soon). Gifts under the threshold with no letter are not listed. `exports/` holds
a Little Green Light gifts export.
