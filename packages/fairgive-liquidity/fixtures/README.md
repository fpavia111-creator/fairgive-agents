# Fixtures

A fictional balance sheet with eleven asset accounts and 1,200,000 of yearly operating
expenses in the settings. Planted: donor-restricted cash marked as available within a year
(restricted_marked_available, left out), board-designated investments left out by the
settings (board_designated_excluded), long-term pledges (receivable_beyond_year) and an
account with no category (no_category). Time-restricted grants receivable due within the
year count as available; prepaid expenses and equipment are not financial assets.
