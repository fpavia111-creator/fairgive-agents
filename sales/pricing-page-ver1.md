# Fairgive pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (Raiser's Edge NXT or Financial Edge NXT, Salesforce NPSP, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Little Green Light, Neon, Givebutter, Classy, PayPal, Stripe, QuickBooks Online, Aplos or Sage Intacct), set your thresholds and policies, run the first reports with you and train one person on your team.

Prices are set at or below the other Fair Agents families because many nonprofits, churches and associations run with a part-time bookkeeper and a small budget. Accounting firms and fiscal sponsors that serve many nonprofits have their own partner rollout. Kurt approved using these proposals.

## Support after setup

- $150 a month for one pack
- $350 a month for the whole organization
- $90 a month per client organization in a partner rollout

Support covers changes in your exports (a CRM or processor changing its columns), threshold and rate updates you ask for (a new IRS figure, a new indirect rate, a new spending policy), new versions of the agents and a monthly check-in call.

## Included and not included

Included: installation on your computer, mapping your exports, your settings (acknowledgement threshold and sending days, quid pro quo figures, fund and net asset accounts, grant budgets and rates, spending policy, 1099 threshold, policy limits), the first run of every agent in the package with you, one training session, the first reports.

Not included: paying, refunding, charging cards, moving money, posting to your books, changing your CRM, filing a Form 990, a 1099 or anything else with the IRS, a state or a funder, or sending letters (the agents never do these; a person on your team does). Legal, tax, audit or accounting advice. Logging in to your CRM, giving platform, processor, bank or funder portal.

Your files and reports stay on your computer. Donor, member, volunteer, staff and vendor names, e-mails, phones, addresses and taxpayer ids are masked in what the agents send to an AI model; a name typed into a memo or note is sent as written unless it is also in a name column.
